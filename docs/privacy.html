Privacy Policy

Character Counter Pro browser extension. Last updated 17 September 2026.

This policy explains what the Character Counter Pro browser extension does with your information. It is written to be read in full in about two minutes.

The short version

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It collects nothing, transmits nothing, and does not talk to any server. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics.

Who we are

Character Counter Pro is published by Chameleon Partnership. You can reach us at paulodonnell@chameleonpartnership.com.

What the extension does with your text

When you paste or type text into the extension popup, the counting runs locally in your browser. Your text is not uploaded, not stored on any server, and not shared with anyone.

What is stored, and where

The extension uses the browser's own storage, which stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

WhatWhereWhy
Your preferences, such as default platform preset, reading and speaking speed, draft memory and themeBrowser sync storage, tied to your browser profileSo the extension opens with your settings
The current draft text, only if you leave draft memory enabledBrowser local storage on your deviceSo closing the popup does not lose your work

You can clear either at any time by turning draft memory off in the extension settings, by clearing the extension's data from your browser, or by removing the extension.

The page checker

The page checker runs only after you click the Check this page button. At that moment the extension reads the page title and the meta description from the tab you are viewing, and shows you their length and a search result preview. It does not modify the page, and the values it reads are not transmitted anywhere. The extension cannot read pages unless you click the button on them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information of any kind.
  • We do not collect your browsing history.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not make any network requests. The extension has no server to send anything to.
  • We do not use remote code. All code is contained within the installed extension package.
  • We do not sell, rent or transfer data to third parties, because we do not have any.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionReason
storageSaves your preferences and, if enabled, your current draft locally so you do not lose work.
activeTabAllows the page checker to access the current tab, and only after you click the button.
scriptingRuns a small read-only script on the current tab to read the page title and meta description. It does not alter the page.

Children

The extension is a general purpose writing tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the extension ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Because the extension currently makes no network requests and collects no data, we do not expect material changes.
